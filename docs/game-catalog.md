# Game catalog: research notes (2026-11-20)

These notes cover the games from the Netflix series 데블스 플랜 / The Devil's Plan, across S1 (2023), S2 *Death Room* (2025) and S3 *Dead Again* (2026, episodes 1–10 only).
All rules here are paraphrased from secondary sources: English Wikipedia, S3 episode transcripts on scrapsfromtheloft.com, Korean news, and namu.wiki search snippets. namu.wiki itself blocks automated reading, so Korean game names are known for S3 only.

Confidence levels:
- **High**: specific rules with numbers, enough to implement.
- **Med**: the core mechanic is known, but some numbers are missing or conflict.
- **Low**: a one-line description only.

---

## Season 1 (12 players)

Format: every player starts with 1 Piece. A player with 0 Pieces is eliminated. After each Main Match, the 2 players with the fewest Pieces go to prison. Prize Matches are co-op games that grow the prize pot.

### Main Matches
| # | Game | Players | Core rules | Conf. |
|---|------|---------|-----------|-------|
| 1 | Virus Game | 12, hidden roles | Roles: 2 Terrorists (each holds a bullet and a virus that spreads by seat), 1 Fanatic (wants to die early), 1 Officer (1 bullet per round), 5 Citizens (one secretly carries the antidote), 2 Researchers (build the cure), 1 Journalist (investigates one player per round). No voting. Terrorists win by killing; citizens win by curing. | Med |
| 2 | Rules Race | 12 | Each player writes personal rules for a board race; shorter rules move earlier in turn order. Die faces: 1,1,2,3,prison,escape. Offices on the board grant an escape card, a reroll, or a change to the group rule. Pieces: 1st +3, 2nd +2, 3rd +1; 10th −1, 11th −3, 12th −5. | Med |
| 3 | Secret Number | 12 | Each player has a secret number from 1–100. Private-room tickets reveal combined info about a pair of players. Score by guessing others' numbers and keeping yours hidden. | Low-Med |
| 4 | Zoo | n | Shared 5×5 grid of animal tiles. Each player has a secret 3-animal sequence and a condition card (animal A must outnumber animal B). Chip auctions decide who places tiles. | Low-Med |
| 5 | Laying Grass | n | 30×30 grid. Players take turns placing polyominoes that touch their own territory and nobody else's. Enclosing key squares unlocks steal or block actions. Score = largest complete square in your territory. | Med |
| 6 | Equation High-Low | n | Pieces convert to chips. Players build equations from number and operator cards, bet High (closest to 20) or Low (closest to 1), and are eliminated when out of chips, until 3 remain. | Med |

### Prize Matches (co-op)
| Game | Core rules | Conf. |
|------|-----------|-------|
| Cooperative Puzzle | Players rotate past a shape-fitting puzzle on a spinning platform, 5 s each turn, and must complete 10 rounds. | Med |
| Fragments of Memory | Study a scene, then answer 10 questions one player at a time. Each player either answers or passes, and one wrong answer fails the group. | Med |
| Word Tower | Use every letter block to spell N English words in a category (N = number of players). | Med |
| Scale Game | Groups deduce the weights (1–20 g) of 5 blocks with a virtual balance. | Med |
| Montage | Faces flash for 3 s each. Buzz on the first repeat, with a limited number of wrong buzzes. | Med |
| Four-Player Three-in-a-Row | Tic-tac-toe variant against a hidden 4th player. Block that player and identify who it is. | Low-Med |

### Prison hidden stage
- **Secret Chamber / Blind Gomoku**: open a 4-digit safe from a riddle (answer: 2024). Inside, play Gomoku with face-down tiles and remember which tiles are yours. (Med)

### Final (1v1, best of 3)
- **Nine Men's Morris**: classic public-domain game.
- **Hexagon**: pick 3 of 19 numbers on a hex board that sum to a target. Memory and arithmetic. (Low)
- A third game was planned but not played.

## Season 2 — Death Room (14 players)

Format: after each Main Match, the top half sleeps in the living space and the bottom half goes to prison. Prison players play a nightly Prison Match in the Death Room, and at least one of them is eliminated. A player with fewer than 1 Piece is eliminated.

### Main Matches
| # | Game | Core rules | Conf. |
|---|------|-----------|-------|
| 1 | Crooked Cops | Subway-map board with 176 stations; Pieces are hidden at 28 of them. Thieves move 2 stations per round to collect Pieces. Cops play in Red, Blue and Green teams and get radio clues. 2 corrupt cops secretly help the thieves. | Med |
| 2 | Unknown | Race a cube to the center of a numbered board while discovering hidden rules. First to the center +3 Pieces; everyone else is ranked by square. | Low-Med |
| 3 | Halloween Monster | Alliances of up to 3 bid victory points to defeat monster cards. Points are split evenly within an alliance; the top scorers share 6 Pieces. Has a secret rule about attack position. | Med |
| 4 | Treasure Island | 81-square board with fences, water and walls. Buy arrow tiles at auction to connect red dots and open 10 clue boxes. Find the treasure box to steal 4 Pieces. Players at ≤20 points lose Pieces. | Med |
| 5 | Balance Mancala | Radial mancala that builds 3 colored scores; your score is the lowest of the three. The lowest scorer gives 4 Pieces to the highest, and ≤15 points loses half your Pieces. | Med |
| 6 | Doubt and Bet | Each player holds 5 colored cards. Bet on how many of a color exist across all hands; the next player raises or challenges. Liar's-dice style, staked with Pieces and slot chips. | Med-High |

### Prison Matches
| # | Game | Core rules | Conf. |
|---|------|-----------|-------|
| 1 | Remove One | Cards 1–8: each round play 2 secretly, then remove one. Lowest unique number wins a token. Decks refresh every 6 rounds; the cycle leader is safe. 18 rounds. | High |
| 2 | Time Auction | 10-minute time bank over 19 rounds. Hold a button; the longest hold wins the round token. Most tokens +1 Piece; fewest tokens eliminated. | High |
| 3 | Sniper Hold'em | Hold'em with 4 community cards. Start with 60 chips; need 75 to survive. "Snipe": name a hand to disqualify it. | Med-High |
| 4 | Triple Dice | 9 visible dice plus 2 hidden (red, blue). Make a 3-die combo, ranked triple > straight > double > single. Round points 6/3/1/0. A correct score prediction doubles your points; correctly predicting 0 earns 40. | High |
| 5 | Wall Go | Move counters on a Go-like board and build a wall after every move. Largest enclosed territory wins; the other two play a rematch and the smaller territory is eliminated. | Med (S3 has full rules) |
| 6 | Equation Pyramid | Pick 3 of 10 numbered tiles and combine them with arithmetic to hit a target. Scored on accuracy and speed. | Med |

### Final (1v1, best of 3)
- **Big Small**: the dealer shows a card from 1–10; the opponent bets whether a face-down card is higher or lower. (Med)
- **Bagh Chal**: classic public-domain Nepali tiger-and-goats game, played twice at once with swapped sides. (High, known game)
- **Question and Truth**: each player secretly arranges 8 cards; spend 10 tokens on questions to deduce the opponent's full arrangement. (Low-Med)

### Hidden stages
- **Knight's Tour** on 6×6, 7×7 and 8×8 boards. (High, known puzzle)
- **Prison well**: press bricks whose names spell FIND THE KEY. (Low)

## Season 3 — Dead Again (16 players, episodes 1–10; episodes 11–14 release 2026-11-24)

Format: losing all Pieces no longer eliminates you; only game results do. The Main Match ranking splits players between the living space and prison, and last place is eliminated. The Main Match winner gets the Token of Alliance, which pulls one player out of prison. Eliminated players can choose Exile (유배지, max 4 players), where Exile matches remove one more player each time. Two ways back from Exile: the Divine Test, or winning the final Exile match on Day 6. Prize Tokens are worth ₩100M each, up to 5.

### Main Matches
| # | Game | Core rules | Conf. |
|---|------|-----------|-------|
| 1 | 양자택일 Either Or | 10 binary choices spread over 4 mini-games: Count the Cubes, Count the Rectangles (find number-grid rectangles that sum to a target), Lay the Grass (polyominoes on a 114-square park) and 기억 포커 Memory Poker (memorize a 4×4 grid, then build a 5-card hand). Ends in a ladder playoff for the bottom ranks. | High |
| 2 | 미스터리 거래소 Mystery Stock Market | ₩100k starting cash. Fictional stocks start at ₩10k (floor ₩100); each follows a hidden price rule revealed at rounds 5, 10, 15 and 20. Each player can apply "Secret" to one stock once. Pieces buy cash or hints. Pieces awarded at asset thresholds. (Sources conflict: 7 or 10 stocks, 20 or 24 rounds.) | High |
| 3 | 보석 암시장 Gem Black Market | 13 cards: diamond, gold, silver and copper of various values, plus 2 bombs (−6, −4). Each player makes 2 exchanges per round with another player, the buyer row or the trash. Score = your card value × the number of matching gem types in the buyer row. Each player has a secret buddy (마니또), and the final score is multiplied by the buddy's total. 3 rounds. | High |
| 4 | 경매 빙고 Auction Bingo | 3×3 card of shapes (triangle, circle, star). Over 7 rounds, secretly pick a shape box, then bid unbreakable coins (1,1,1,2,2,2,3,3,5,10); coins are spent win or lose. The top bid wins as many shapes as there were bidders. Winners stay hidden. Rank by bingos, then squares, then coins. | High |

### Prison Matches
| # | Game | Core rules | Conf. |
|---|------|-----------|-------|
| 1 | 무거운 13 Heavy 13 | Each round, play a card (0–6) and a chip (R/G/B ×2 each, plus 1 rainbow). Rocks of that value go on the matching-color scale, in order: rainbow first, then highest card. Pushing a scale over 13 (or 12, sources conflict) makes you take penalty points equal to the rocks cleared. 14 rounds; hand refresh at round 7. | High |
| 2 | 러시안 룰렛 Russian Roulette | 6 seats around a 6-chamber gun with 2 bullets. Credits buy actions: public peek (1), private peek (2), rotate 1 step (3), rotate 2 steps (4), secret swap (6). The gun fires when 3 players are broke. | High |
| 3 | 돼지 저금통 Piggy Bank | Add 1–5 black or white coins to a bank and pass it on. The receiver must declare the count of one color, alternating colors. Others can buzz to doubt: ±1. First to 5 points. Reset and "name all counts" options. | High |
| 4 | 벽바둑 2 Wall Go II | Square counters move 1–2 straight; round counters move 1 step plus an optional turn. Build a wall after every move. 1 wall-breaker per player. 90 s per turn. Largest territory wins; 2-player final. | High |
